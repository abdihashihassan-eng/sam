Siyaasadda Soo Celinta & Beddelka (Return & Refund Policy)

La cusboonaysiiyay / Last updated: Abriil 15, 2025

Shirkadda SAHAN DATA waxay ku dadaashaa inay bixiso adeegyo tayo sare leh oo data iyo airtime ah. Si kastaba ha ahaatee, sababtoo ah alaabtu waa kuwo dijitaal ah, siyaasaddeena soo celinta iyo beddelka waa mid xaddidan.

📌 1. Alaabaha Aan La Soo Celin Karin

Data bundles, airtime (lacagta hadalka), iyo digital vouchers lama soo celin karo marka dalabka si guul leh loo wada shaqeeyay oo USSD code la soo saaray. Markaas waxay noqotaa mid kama dambeys ah.

🔄 2. Xaaladaha Laga Yaabo in La Tixgeliyo Soo Celinta

Soo celinta waxaa laga yaabaa in la tixgeliyo oo kaliya haddii ay dhacdo mid ka mid ah xaaladahan:

  • Fashil tignoolajiyadeed: Adeegga lama bixin (tusaale: USSD code ma shaqayn, isla markaana shirkadda isgaadhsiintu ay xaqiijiso in aan la isticmaalin).
  • Dalab labanlaab ah: Alaab la mid ah ayaa si qalad ah laba jeer loo iibsaday 5 daqiiqo gudahood.
  • Alaab qalad ah la bixiyay: Alaabta aad heshay way ka duwan tahay kii aad dooratay.

⏱️ 3. Xaddiga Waqtiga ee Codsiga

Dhammaan codsiyada soo celinta waa in la soo gudbiyaa 24 saacadood gudahood laga bilaabo markii dalabka la sameeyay. Codsyo ka dib 24 saacadood soo gaara lama aqbali doono.

📝 4. Sidee Looga Codsan Karaa Soo Celinta

La xiriir kooxda taageerada iyada oo loo marayo WhatsApp (+252 61 253 8844) ama iimayl support@sahandata.com adigoo la socodsiiya nambarka dalabkaaga iyo sharaxaad faahfaahsan. Kooxdeenu waxay dib u eegi doontaa, waxayna ku soo jawaabi doontaa 2 maalmood oo shaqo gudahood.

💳 5. Habka Soo Celinta

Haddii la oggolaado, lacagta soo celinta waxaa lagu soo celin doonaa si la mid ah habka bixinta ee la adeegsaday markii la iibsaday (EVC Plus, eDahab, ama Jeeb). Habka soo celintu wuxuu qaadan karaa 5–7 maalmood oo shaqo.

🔒 6. Eedaynta Khidmadda Bangiga (Chargebacks)

Haddii eedayn la bilaabo iyada oo aan hore loo soo gaadhsin taageerada, waxay keeni doontaa hakad degdeg ah oo koontadaaga iyo luminta dhammaan dhibcaha bonus-ka iyo abaalmarinnada aad hore u heshay.

At SAHAN DATA, we strive to provide high‑quality data and airtime services. However, due to the nature of digital products, our return and refund policy is limited.

📌 1. Non‑Refundable Products

Data bundles, airtime top‑ups, and digital vouchers are non‑refundable once the order has been successfully processed and the USSD code has been generated. The transaction is considered final.

🔄 2. Refund Exceptions

A refund may be considered only in the following cases:

  • Technical failure: The service was not delivered (e.g., USSD code did not work, and the mobile operator confirms no usage).
  • Duplicate order: The same product was accidentally purchased twice within 5 minutes.
  • Wrong product delivered: The product you received differs from what you selected.

⏱️ 3. Time Limit for Claims

All refund requests must be submitted within 24 hours of the order time. Requests made after 24 hours will not be accepted.

📝 4. How to Request a Refund

Contact our support team via WhatsApp (+252 61 253 8844) or email support@sahandata.com with your order number and a detailed explanation. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days.

💳 5. Refund Process

If approved, the refund will be issued to the same payment method used for the purchase (EVC Plus, eDahab, or Jeeb). Processing may take 5–7 business days.

🔒 6. Chargebacks

Any chargeback initiated without prior contact will result in immediate suspension of your account and forfeiture of any bonus points or rewards.
